COMPUTER VISION & AUDIO AI Dual Biometrics

SmartClass – AI-Powered Smart Attendance Management System

A deployed web-based biometric attendance system with role-segregated teacher and student portals, replacing manual roll calls with continuous multi-modal face and voice verification.

Face Biometrics with dlib (128-D Embeddings): Employs ±15° micro-tilt passes for natural classroom photography; matches exclusively against students enrolled in the selected subject roster to prevent cross-class false positives.
Passive FFT Anti-Spoofing: Fast Fourier Transform frequency domain filter detects screen glare, rectangular device bezels, and Moiré patterns, blocking electronic photo/video spoofing attempts.
Voice Biometrics (256-D Speaker Vectors): Integrates Resemblyzer with Librosa audio segmentation, splitting classroom audio and authenticating speakers via cosine similarity thresholds.
Dynamic QR Enrollment (Segno): Auto-enrollment links with student login verification, combined with real-time teacher manual override reviews before database commit.
Resilient Supabase Backend: Configured exponential-backoff retry logic for reliable database transactions, bcrypt password hashing, and Python version compatibility patches.

MACHINE LEARNING & CREDIT RISK 95.8% Test Accuracy

LoanSense AI – Loan Approval Prediction & Credit Risk Analytics

An end-to-end automated machine learning credit underwriting platform that predicts loan approval probabilities, calculates risk tiers, and surfaces dynamic sensitivity dashboards.

High-Performance Gradient Boosting: Tuned ensemble classifier achieving 0.942 F1-score, 0.991 ROC-AUC, and 95.8% accuracy on held-out test datasets.
Rigorous 5-Fold Stratified Benchmark: Systematically evaluated 7 classifiers; tree ensembles consistently outperformed linear baselines by ~0.16 F1 margin.
Leak-Free Scikit-Learn Pipeline: Engineered modular ColumnTransformers with median/mode imputation, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding serialized cleanly via joblib.
Explainability & Fairness Verification: In-depth EDA demonstrated Debt-to-Income ratio (~52%) and Credit Score (~38%) govern ~90% of approval decisions; verified protected demographics had near-zero importance.
Interactive Streamlit Intelligence Hub: Built live risk gauge, Plotly EDA visualization suite, model leaderboard, and custom cutoff sensitivity simulation.
